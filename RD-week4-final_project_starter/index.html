Final Project

Colours can influence how people interpret a place, product, or message. Red is often used to suggest energy, urgency, or passion, while orange and yellow can feel warm, lively, and optimistic. Green is commonly associated with nature and growth, and blue can communicate calm or trust. These associations vary across cultures and personal experience, so colour psychology is a guide to perception rather than a universal rule.

Colour meanings appear throughout everyday life: traffic signals use red, amber, and green to make instructions easy to recognize, and brands choose palettes to shape the mood of packaging and websites. Clothing, interior design, maps, and public signs also use colour to express identity, organize information, or draw attention. Looking at each swatch is a reminder that context helps determine what a colour communicates.
